Verified assembly details used:
- Clean support material before assembly and add one 3-pin cable after placing each motor.
- Joint 1: top horn center M3x6; no center screw on bottom horn; motor fixed with four M2x6; holder with two M2x6; shoulder connected with M3x6 screws.
- Joint 2: motor fixed with four M2x6; upper arm attached with four M3x6 on each side.
- Joint 3: motor fixed with four M2x6; forearm attached with four M3x6 on each side.
- Joint 4: holder slides over motor; motor fixed with four M2x6.
- Joint 5: motor fixed with two front M2x6; one horn uses an M3x6 center screw; wrist attaches to motor 4 with M3x6 screws.
- Follower gripper differs from leader handle after the common arm assembly steps.
